UEFA Europa Conference League

The UEFA Europa Conference League (UECL), set to be renamed the UEFA Conference League starting from the 2024–25 season, represents a new chapter in European football. Established by the Union of European Football Associations (UEFA), this annual football competition made its debut in the 2021–22 season and holds the distinction of being the third tier of continental club football in Europe. Structured as a supplementary level beneath the Europa League, the UEFA Europa Conference League is integral to UEFA's vision of providing additional opportunities for eligible European football clubs, particularly those from lower-ranked UEFA member associations. The restructuring of the Europa League, reducing the number of teams in the group stage from 48 to 32, emphasizes the UEFA Europa Conference League's role as the entry point for teams seeking continental competition. Participation in the UEFA Europa Conference League is unique; no teams qualify directly to the group stage. Instead, 10 teams are eliminated in the Europa League play-offs, while the remaining teams join the Europa Conference League qualifiers. This approach ensures a dynamic and competitive mix of clubs from various footballing backgrounds. The significance of the competition extends beyond the pursuit of the trophy. The winners of the UEFA Europa Conference League are not only crowned champions but are also granted a coveted position in the subsequent season's Europa League, further elevating the competition's status. However, if the victorious team qualifies for the UEFA Champions League, they enter the higher-tier competition instead. In its inaugural season, AS Roma emerged as the first winners of the UEFA Europa Conference League, triumphing over Feyenoord with a 1–0 victory in the 2022 final. The subsequent season saw West Ham United claim the title, overcoming Fiorentina with a 2–1 win in the 2023 final. As the UEFA Europa Conference League continues to unfold, it adds a new dimension to the European football landscape. The competition's unique structure, commitment to inclusivity, and the opportunity it provides for clubs to progress to higher levels contribute to the ever-evolving narrative of European football. The impending renaming of the tournament to the UEFA Conference League signals a forward-looking approach, emphasizing its role in shaping the future of continental club football.

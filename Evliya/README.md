# Evliya: Interactive District Discovery Atlas of Turkey

**Evliya** is a web-based interactive map application that allows users to track their travels across Turkey's 973 districts. Discover new places, mark them on your map, and generate a "Discovery Certificate" to share your progress and traveler rank with the world.

**Live Demo:** [https://ismailoksuz.github.io/evliya/](https://ismailoksuz.github.io/evliya/)

---

## Features

* **Interactive Map:** Click to mark districts you have visited on a high-performance Leaflet-based map.
* **Progress Tracking:** Real-time counter showing the percentage of Turkey you have explored.
* **Traveler Ranks:** Earn titles from "Novice Traveler" to "Cihanşümul" based on your discovery count.
* **Shareable Cards:** Generate a custom discovery certificate with your name and rank.
* **Local Storage:** Your data is saved directly on your device, so your progress isn't lost when you refresh.
* **Mobile Friendly:** Optimized for both desktop and mobile browsers.

## Built With

* [Leaflet.js](https://leafletjs.org/) - Interactive maps
* [Html2Canvas](https://html2canvas.hertzen.com/) - Screenshot generation for certificates
* [Mapshaper](https://mapshaper.org/) - Used for GeoJSON optimization
* **CSS3 & Vanilla JS** - For a lightweight and fast user experience

## How to Use

1.  Open the map.
2.  Click on any district to mark it as "Explored".
3.  Use the share button to enter your name and create your traveler certificate.
4.  Download the image or share it directly on social media.
